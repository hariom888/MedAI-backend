**Severity:** Severe

## Summary
Schizophrenia is a severe condition with a lifelong (though about one third recover completely after one episode) course. Core symptoms include: hallucinations, delusions, speech. Secondary symptoms may include: movements. Risk groups: s, t, a, r, t. Emergency warning signs: m, e, n, t, a.
