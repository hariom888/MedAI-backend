# Leptospirosis

**Severity:** Mild_to_severe  
**Duration:** Acute (1-2 weeks); severe or complicated (several weeks)  
**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Leptospirosis is a mild_to_severe condition with a acute (1-2 weeks); severe or complicated (several weeks) course. Core symptoms include: muscle, headache, nausea, fever. Secondary symptoms may include: vomiting. Risk groups: f, a, r, m, e. Emergency warning signs: y, e, l, l, o.
