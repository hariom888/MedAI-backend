# Psoriasis

**Severity:** Mild_to_severe  
**Duration:** Lifelong condition; symptoms fluctuate over time  
**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Psoriasis is a mild_to_severe condition with a lifelong condition; symptoms fluctuate over time course. Core symptoms include: red or pink patches of skin covered with silvery scales, dry, cracked skin that may bleed, itching, burning, or soreness around patches. Secondary symptoms may include: thickened or pitted nails, swollen and stiff joints (psoriatic arthritis), patches on scalp, elbows, knees, or lower back. Risk groups: i, n, d, i, v. Emergency warning signs: w, i, d, e, s.
