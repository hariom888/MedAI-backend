# Atopic Eczema

**Severity:** Mild_to_moderate  
**Duration:** Chronic with periodic flare-ups  
**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Atopic Eczema is a mild_to_moderate condition with a chronic with periodic flare-ups course. Core symptoms include: intense itching (pruritus), dry, cracked skin. Secondary symptoms may include: red or inflamed patches, thickened or scaly skin in chronic cases, oozing or crusting in severe cases. Risk groups: c, h, i, l, d. Emergency warning signs: w, i, d, e, s.
