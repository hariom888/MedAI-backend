## Summary
Rheumatoid Arthritis is a moderate_to_severe condition with a chronic lifelong condition with relapsing and remitting flares course. Core symptoms include: stiffness, fatigue, fever. Secondary symptoms may include: weakness, walking. Risk groups: w, o, m, e, n. Emergency warning signs: c, h, e, s, t.
