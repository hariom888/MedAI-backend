# Eczema — Part 1: Core Clinical Profile

**Severity:** Mild_to_moderate  
**Duration:** Long-lasting disease, may get better or worse over time, often improves as children grow older; chronic with periodic flare-ups

## Summary
Eczema is a mild_to_moderate condition with a long-lasting disease, may get better or worse over time, often improves as children grow older; chronic with periodic flare-ups course. Core symptoms include: dry skin, itchy skin, red rashes, swelling and increased redness after scratching. Secondary symptoms may include: intense itching (pruritus), cracked skin, red or inflamed patches, thickened or scaly skin in chronic cases, oozing or crusting in severe cases. Risk groups: b, a, b, i, e. Emergency warning signs: w, i, d, e, s.
