# Appendicitis

**Severity:** Severe  
**Duration:** Acute condition (progresses quickly over hours to days)  
**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Appendicitis is a severe condition with a acute condition (progresses quickly over hours to days) course. Core symptoms include: nausea, vomiting, coughing, walking. Secondary symptoms may include: constipation, fever. Risk groups: m, o, s, t,  . Emergency warning signs: s, u, d, d, e.
