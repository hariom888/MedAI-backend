# Fibromyalgia — Part 1: Core Clinical Profile

**Severity:** Mild_to_severe  
**Duration:** Chronic long-term condition, often months to years

## Summary
Fibromyalgia is a mild_to_severe condition with a chronic long-term condition, often months to years course. Core symptoms include: memory, jaw, muscle, stiffness, fatigue, light, odors, numbness. Secondary symptoms may include: anxiety, balance, depression, tingling. Risk groups: w, o, m, e, n. Emergency warning signs: s, e, v, e, r.
