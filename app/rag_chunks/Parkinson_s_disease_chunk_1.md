# Parkinson's disease — Part 1: Core Clinical Profile

**Severity:** Moderate_to_severe  
**Duration:** Chronic lifelong condition with gradual progression over years to decades

## Summary
Parkinson's disease is a moderate_to_severe condition with a chronic lifelong condition with gradual progression over years to decades course. Core symptoms include: fingers, foot, jaw, bradykinesia, muscle, rigidity, stiffness, painful. Secondary symptoms may include: writing, depression, constipation. Risk groups: a, d, u, l, t. Emergency warning signs: f, r, e, q, u.
