# Low Back Pain

**Severity:** Mild_to_severe

**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Low Back Pain is a mild_to_severe condition with a acute days to 6 weeks, subacute 6-12 weeks, chronic over 12 weeks course. Core symptoms include: muscle, stiffness, movements, numbness, weakness. Secondary symptoms may include: walking, fever. Risk groups: a, d, u, l, t. Emergency warning signs: l, o, s, s, .
