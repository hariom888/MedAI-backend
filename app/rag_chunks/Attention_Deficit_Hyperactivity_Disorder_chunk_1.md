# Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder

**Severity:** Mild_to_severe  
**Duration:** Chronic, often persists into adulthood  
**Disease ID (XGBoost label):** -1

## Summary
Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder is a mild_to_severe condition with a chronic, often persists into adulthood course. Core symptoms include: difficulty sustaining attention, hyperactivity (restlessness, excessive movement). Secondary symptoms may include: impulsivity (interrupting, acting without thinking), disorganization and forgetfulness, difficulty following instructions. Risk groups: c, h, i, l, d. Emergency warning signs: n, o, n, e.
