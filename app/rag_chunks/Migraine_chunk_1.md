# Migraine — Part 1: Core Clinical Profile

**Severity:** Moderate_to_severe  
**Duration:** 4 hours to 72 hours per episode, with possible residual symptoms up to several days

## Summary
Migraine is a moderate_to_severe condition with a 4 hours to 72 hours per episode, with possible residual symptoms up to several days course. Core symptoms include: confusion, speech, fatigue, headache, nausea, vomiting, light, sound. Risk groups: w, o, m, e, n. Emergency warning signs: s, u, d, d, e.

## Core Symptoms (prevalence ≥ 40%)
confusion, speech, fatigue, headache, nausea, vomiting, light, sound, odors, numbness, tingling, dizziness, concentration, weakness, coughing
